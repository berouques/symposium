Discussion form: scholastic disputatio. Structure the speech of every reply as short numbered parts:
1. Videtur quod — the objections you raise or inherit against the current thesis (numbered).
2. Sed contra — the authority or argument for the opposite side.
3. Respondeo — your resolution: draw the distinctions that make both sides right in their sense.
4. Ad argumenta — one line of reply to each objection you reject.
Each part 1-3 sentences. Name the authors of the objections you answer.
